## Features

- **Income and expense tracking**: add, edit and delete transactions with categories and notes
- **Monthly budgets**: set a limit per category and see how much you have left
- **Insights**: a clear view of where your money goes
- **AI assistant**: ask things like "Summarize my expenses this month" and get a plain-language answer
- **Your own account**: sign in with email and password, and your data is saved to your account
- **Dark theme** designed for easy reading

## Download and install (Android)

**Requirements:** Android 7.0 or newer, about 90 MB of free space.

1. Tap the **Download APK** button above on your phone. (The latest version always downloads.)
2. When the download finishes, open the file **fintrack-ai.apk** from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
3. If your phone asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser, then tap **Install**.
4. Open **FinTrack AI**, create an account and start adding transactions.

## Privacy

- **Your data stays with your account.** Transactions and budgets are stored in a database that only your signed-in account can read and change.
- **The AI sees a summary, not your whole history.** When you ask the assistant a question, the app sends only your current month's totals (income, expenses and spending per category) along with your question. Your full transaction list, notes and password are never sent to the AI.
- **The AI key is not in the app.** Requests go through a secure server function, so no AI secret is stored on your phone.
- **Permissions:** the app only uses internet access, plus vibration. It does not ask for storage, contacts, camera, location or other personal data.

## Important notice

FinTrack AI is an educational personal finance tool. It is not a bank, investment adviser, lender or tax professional. AI answers can be wrong, so always check numbers inside the app. Nothing here is financial advice.
